## Goal

Complete the creative-preproduction workflow with production-oriented text settings and traceable shot plans.

## Acceptance criteria

- Confirmed scripts generate role, scene, prop, and global-style text settings with all required fields.
- Users edit, confirm, lock, and locally regenerate individual setting assets.
- Confirmed settings and scripts generate shots based on visual focus, action, emotion, and information change.
- Each shot includes all fields specified in `docs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md` and references script, source, and setting assets.
- Storyboard mode preserves the same canonical selection and approval state as trace and review modes.
- Episode shot duration totals remain within 10% of target duration or show a blocking validation result.
