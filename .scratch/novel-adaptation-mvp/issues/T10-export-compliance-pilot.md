## Goal

Close the MVP loop with consistent exports, operational safeguards, mobile review, and measurable closed-beta readiness.

## Acceptance criteria

- DOCX, XLSX, and versioned JSON exports conform to the documented contract and contain consistent confirmed content.
- Candidate exports, if enabled, are visibly marked unconfirmed.
- Mobile users can view progress, review stages, process suggestions, and receive notifications without full editing.
- Rights declarations, content restrictions, appeals, complaints, sharing suspension, deletion, recycle-bin, backup expiry, and mainland data residency behaviors are testable.
- Free-trial, studio, and advanced-studio entitlements enforce member and credit limits without charging for ordinary editing or export.
- Production observability covers job failures, cost, latency, settlement discrepancies, and compliance actions without logging user manuscripts.
- A release checklist supports invitation of 5–10 studios and measures the agreed completion, reuse, and willingness-to-pay signals.
