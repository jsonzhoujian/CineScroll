## Goal

Validate terminology, workflow fit, three-mode usability, collaboration, export expectations, reuse, and willingness to pay with 5–10 real target studios after a minimally operable end-to-end product exists.

## Deliverables

- Completed anonymous sessions in `docs/research/PROTOTYPE_FINDINGS.md`.
- Prioritized findings linked to requirements and implementation tickets.
- Evidence-based decision on three-mode naming, entry points, and continued retention.

## Acceptance criteria

- At least five eligible 1–10 person studios complete all applicable end-to-end tasks.
- Each session records task outcomes and evidence without including studio identity or manuscript content.
- Findings distinguish comprehension failure, workflow friction, missing capability, and preference.
- Material findings produce a requirement change, a follow-up experiment, or an explicit non-change.
- At least three studios process a second project or chapter within 14 days before paid launch validation is considered successful.
